2. Data Processed by Join

Depending on how you use Join, the following data is processed:

  • Account data: name, email address and a cryptographic hash of your password. The password itself is not stored in plain text.
  • Contact data: names, email addresses and telephone numbers that you enter in the contacts section.
  • Task and project data: task titles, descriptions, due dates, categories, priorities, subtasks, status, assigned contacts and creator information. For requests submitted by email, this may include the sender's name and email address. Temporary notification events may also contain the task title, previous status, new status and creator email address.
  • Session and local data: the displayed user name, login status, a flag recording whether the intro animation has already been shown, and locally cached tasks and contacts.
  • Technical data: when requests are sent to Firebase or the Gemini API, Google may process data such as the IP address, request time, resource identifiers and technical information about the request.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

Account, contact and task data is processed to provide the requested account, Kanban board, contact management and synchronization functions. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. The technically necessary session and local storage is used to keep the app functional and the user signed in. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and, where applicable, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the reliable and secure operation of the application.

For tasks submitted by email, the creator's email address is also used to send processing confirmations and notifications about later status changes.

Join does not use the data for advertising and does not sell personal data.

4. Firebase Realtime Database

Join uses Firebase Realtime Database, a service provided by Google, to store and synchronize account data, contacts and tasks. For users in the European Economic Area, the relevant Google contracting entity is generally Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google processes customer data on our behalf under its Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms.

The Realtime Database used by Join is configured for the europe-west1 region, which Google identifies as Belgium. Google states that Firebase Realtime Database data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Google may also process Firebase service and diagnostic data on global infrastructure. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, Google relies on recognized transfer mechanisms such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Further information is available in Google's Firebase privacy and security information, Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

5. AI Analysis of Email Requests

When a stakeholder sends a request to the dedicated Join email address, the email subject, email body and received date are transmitted through the Google Gemini API. This data is used only to generate a concise task title and determine the task category, priority and any deadline stated in the email. The sender's name and email address are not included in the AI prompt unless the sender writes them in the subject or body.

Join uses the Gemini API free tier for this educational project. Google states that content submitted through the free tier may be used to improve its products. Therefore, stakeholder emails must not contain confidential, sensitive or business-critical information.

Further information is available in Google's Gemini API pricing and data-use information and Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

Join sets a technically necessary loggedInUser cookie for up to one hour and uses sessionStorage to retain the displayed user for the current browser session. Tasks and contacts may also be cached in the browser's IndexedDB database so that the board can work reliably.

These browser storage mechanisms are required for the functions requested by the user. You can remove them using your browser settings; doing so may sign you out or remove locally cached app data.

7. Recipients

Personal data is disclosed only where necessary to operate Join, in particular to Google as the provider of Firebase Realtime Database and the Gemini API, or where disclosure is required by law. No data is shared with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

8. Retention

Account data remains stored until you request deletion or the service is discontinued. Contacts and tasks remain in Firebase until they are deleted in Join or a deletion request is fulfilled. Browser session data is removed when the session ends or when you log out; the login cookie expires after one hour. Locally cached data remains until it is overwritten, deleted through the app or removed in the browser settings. Processing and possible retention by Google for Gemini free-tier requests is described in section 5. Statutory retention obligations remain unaffected.

11. Security

Join transmits data to Firebase and the Gemini API over HTTPS, and Google states that Firebase Realtime Database data is encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than in plain text. Nevertheless, no transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Please do not enter sensitive personal data in task descriptions, contact fields or stakeholder emails unless it is necessary.
